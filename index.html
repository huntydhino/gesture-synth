    Gesture Synth Guide

    Gesture Synth is an interactive musical instrument created by Eric Wei. Use your hands to create chords, melodies, and sounds in real-time!

    Left Hand

    Tilt
    Inward → Major
    Outward → Minor

    Fingers (Scale Degree)
    1 → I
    2 → II
    3 → III
    4 → IV
    5 → V
    Index + Pinky → VI
    Index + Pinky + Thumb → VII

    Right Hand

    Fingers (Chord Quality)
    1 → Root Position
    2 → 1st Inversion
    3 → Major/Minor 7th
    4 → Dominant/Diminished 7th

    Octave
    Thumb In → higher octave
    Thumb Out → lower octave

    Tilt
    Inward → More Filter
    Outward → Less Filter

    Height
    Higher → Louder
    Lower → Softer

    Head Tracking (Face Mesh)

    Tilt Left → Pan Audio Left & Pink Dots
    Tilt Right → Pan Audio Right & Green Dots
    Level / Center → Balanced Stereo & Cyan Dots
